# Nine-Studio Orchestration

## Goal

Use nine specialized AI Jarvis Creative Creation Studios as a controlled production line for next-generation AI prototypes. The studios are isolated configurations, not independent legal persons or uncontrolled self-replicating agents.

## Flow

1. Discovery produces an evidence-backed problem.
2. Blueprint converts the problem into contracts, acceptance tests, and risk boundaries.
3. Coding implements a minimal prototype on an isolated branch.
4. Diagnostics reproduces behavior and verifies tests.
5. Security independently examines misuse, data, permissions, and recovery.
6. MemPalace records approved evidence and lineage.
7. Creative Experience produces accessible interfaces, teaching material, and demonstrations.
8. Business validates usefulness, delivery costs, and revenue assumptions.
9. Integration assembles a release candidate and requests owner approval.

A studio may return work to an earlier studio when evidence fails. It may not skip Diagnostics, Security, Provenance, or owner approval.

## Anti-recursion controls

- A studio cannot create or activate another studio.
- Only an owner-approved blueprint may add or change a studio configuration.
- Studio-generated prompts and code remain untrusted until reviewed and tested.
- A prototype cannot change its permission, memory, evaluation, or promotion policy.
- No prototype may execute another prototype’s proposed actions automatically.
- Maximum improvement generations per experiment: three.
- Every generation receives a new version, bundle ID, hashes, test results, and rollback record.
- Failed candidates are quarantined as evidence, not promoted.
- Promotion is from candidate to approved baseline; production mutation in place is prohibited.

## Parallel work

The following work may run concurrently after an approved blueprint:

- Coding implementation and Creative Experience mockups.
- Diagnostic test design and Security threat-case design.
- Provenance preparation and Business experiment design.

Results rejoin at Integration only after their required gates pass.

## Conflict resolution

When studios disagree:

1. deterministic test or runtime evidence;
2. explicit product requirement;
3. security and privacy constraint;
4. documented baseline behavior;
5. independent review;
6. Charles's decision.

A majority of AI opinions is not proof.

## First fleet mission

Create and evaluate **Jarvis Repository Doctor v0.1**, limited to read-only analysis of one low-risk public repository. It may report findings and propose changes but may not edit, push, merge, deploy, access credentials, or contact third parties.
