# AI Jarvis Affiliate & Business Systems — One-Page Plan

## Purpose
Build a coordinated business system that helps small businesses find financing and build stronger digital systems, while also growing practical technology education through AI Evolutionary Evolutions.

## Problem
Small business owners can struggle to find suitable working capital, equipment financing, lines of credit, term/SBA loans, invoice factoring, or commercial real-estate financing. They may also lack the web presence and technical systems needed to operate and grow. Separately, aspiring builders need practical, accessible ways to learn Linux, AI, programming, and blockchain.

## Solution
- **Lippytm / GetBizFunds:** Connect U.S. businesses with financing options and guide them toward an application. The current site presents multiple financing categories and describes the business as an intermediary, not the lender.
- **AI Evolutionary Evolutions:** Offer hands-on technology learning, build sessions, projects, a learning library, and a founding membership.
- **Hostinger affiliate:** Recommend Hostinger services when they genuinely fit a business or builder’s website and infrastructure needs; clearly disclose affiliate links and do not imply financing approval or guaranteed results.
- **AI Jarvis:** Coordinate the work through GitHub—plans, tasks, content drafts, approved link records, and performance reviews—with human approval before publishing, external messages, financial claims, or other sensitive actions.

## Target customers
1. U.S. small-business owners seeking capital for operations, equipment, or expansion and practical systems to run their business.
2. New and developing builders who want practical Linux, AI, programming, or blockchain experience and a supportive learning community.

Keep these as **separate audience journeys** under one business umbrella: finance prospects should land in the financing funnel; learners and builders should land in the education/community funnel. Introduce Hostinger only where relevant to the visitor’s goal.

## Revenue model
- Financing-related referral or broker compensation, if applicable under partner agreements.
- Hostinger affiliate commissions on eligible referrals.
- The AI Evolutionary Evolutions site currently advertises a $5/month founding-builder membership.
- Potential sales of educational ebooks, audiobooks, video books, or other learning products; confirm which are ready to sell before promoting them.

## Key measures
Track qualified financing inquiries, completed applications, partner-approved/funded outcomes, and revenue per referral; Hostinger link clicks, conversions, and commissions; membership sign-ups, retention, and learning engagement; and content output, lead sources, and Jarvis workflow completion. Establish baselines before setting numerical targets.

## Competitive advantage
A practical combination of business-funding pathways and hands-on technical education, supported by a builder-focused brand and a GitHub-governed AI operations system. Jarvis can reduce coordination work while approval gates preserve human control and trust.

## Jarvis operating guardrails
Use the designated private GitHub control repository as the source of truth. Automate low-risk research, organization, draft creation, and reporting. Require approval before publishing or sending outreach, editing money-related or eligibility claims, sharing customer information, changing secrets or integrations, making purchases, or taking destructive actions. Keep credentials and personally identifying financial documents out of ordinary repository files.
