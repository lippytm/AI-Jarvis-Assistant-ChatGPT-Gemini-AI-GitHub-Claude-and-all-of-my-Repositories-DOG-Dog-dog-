# AI RPG Affiliate Curriculum Integration

## Purpose
Connect the programming, Linux, blockchain, AI-agent, educational-entertainment, and business-development curriculum to transparent resource recommendations without turning the lessons into unverified advertising.

## Program status
- Hostinger Referral membership: active.
- Hostinger Partner approval: not confirmed.
- Hostinger referral URL: pending retrieval and verification.
- CLONECAST program status and link: pending verification.
- Tracked referrals and earnings: none currently recorded.

## Placement rules
1. Teach the learning outcome first.
2. Add a resource card only when the resource genuinely supports the lesson.
3. Keep Hostinger Referral, any separate Hostinger Affiliate enrollment, CLONECAST, and other programs separate.
4. Add a plain-language disclosure beside each verified referral link.
5. Never claim guaranteed income, approval, savings, business growth, or technical results.
6. Keep paid advertising disabled until the free offer is tested and initial revenue exists.

- Audience fit.
- Plain-language purpose.
- Limitations and non-guarantees.

- Owner: Intergalactic Corporate Services LLC.

## Approval gates
- Program identity and terms verified.
- Link retrieved from the correct dashboard.
- Disclosure reviewed.
- Landing page and event tracking tested.
- Human approval recorded before public publishing or paid advertising.
